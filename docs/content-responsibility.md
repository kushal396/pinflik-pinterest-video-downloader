# Content Responsibility

## Access is not ownership

PinFlik provides a technical way to save supported publicly accessible Pinterest media. Downloading a file does not transfer copyright ownership.

## User responsibility

Users are responsible for determining whether they have permission to download, store, reproduce, modify, publish, or commercially use a piece of content.

## Recommended responsible behavior

- Download content you own or are permitted to use.
- Respect creator requests and licenses.
- Give credit where appropriate or required.
- Do not use PinFlik to download private or restricted content.
- Do not assume "publicly visible" means "free of copyright."
- Follow applicable laws and platform terms.

## Copyright and DMCA

PinFlik maintains a public DMCA policy and describes itself as a technical tool for publicly accessible Pinterest media rather than a host of the downloaded files.
