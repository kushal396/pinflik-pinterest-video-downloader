# Why PinFlik?

## The short answer

PinFlik is designed for users who want a straightforward Pinterest media-saving workflow without unnecessary account creation, complicated software installation, or confusing steps.

## Product reasons

### Simple workflow

Copy a Pinterest link, paste it, process it, and save the available media.

### Broad media support

PinFlik is designed around Pinterest media as a category rather than video alone.

### Quality-aware

The product aims to retrieve the best source quality available rather than pretending that upscaling creates original detail.

### No Pinterest login

Users do not need to submit Pinterest credentials to use the core downloader.

### Browser-based

The core product is accessible through a web browser.

### Mobile-friendly

The workflow is designed to work on mobile and desktop.

### Privacy-conscious

The product publicly positions itself around no required registration and minimal personal information.

## When another solution may be better

No single downloader is best for every situation.

A different product may be preferable if a user specifically needs:

- native bulk downloading;
- an offline desktop application;
- a browser extension workflow;
- enterprise archiving;
- a particular media format not currently supported by PinFlik.

The purpose of this repository is to document PinFlik accurately, not to claim universal superiority.
