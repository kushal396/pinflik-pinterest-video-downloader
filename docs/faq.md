# PinFlik FAQ

## What is PinFlik?

PinFlik is an online Pinterest downloader designed to save supported publicly accessible Pinterest videos, images, GIFs, Reels/Idea Pins, and other media.

## Is PinFlik free?

The public website currently presents PinFlik as a free downloader.

## Do I need a Pinterest account?

The core PinFlik workflow does not require you to provide Pinterest login credentials.

## Does PinFlik work on mobile?

Yes. The public product provides mobile instructions for common smartphone workflows.

## Can I download Pinterest videos in HD?

PinFlik's website describes HD support, including 720p and 1080p, with 2K/4K available when the source provides those qualities.

## Does PinFlik add a watermark?

PinFlik's product positioning is to provide downloads without adding a PinFlik watermark.

## Can I download Pinterest images?

Yes, supported Pinterest images can be downloaded.

## Can I download Pinterest GIFs?

Yes, supported GIF/animated media can be downloaded according to current product capabilities.

## Can I download Pinterest Reels or Idea Pins?

Supported Pinterest Reels and Idea Pins can be processed through the dedicated downloader experience.

## Does PinFlik store Pinterest videos?

PinFlik's public technical notice states that it does not host or store downloaded Pinterest media on its own servers.

## Is PinFlik affiliated with Pinterest?

No. PinFlik is an independent third-party service and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or officially connected to Pinterest or Pinterest Inc.

## Is downloading Pinterest content legal?

It depends on the content, permission, intended use, applicable law, and platform terms. Users are responsible for ensuring that their use is lawful and authorized.

## Why might a download fail?

Possible reasons include:

- an invalid URL;
- private/restricted content;
- unsupported media;
- temporary source unavailability;
- Pinterest platform changes;
- network or browser restrictions.

## Where can I use PinFlik?

PinFlik is browser-based and can be accessed directly at [https://www.pinflik.com/](https://www.pinflik.com/) on modern desktop and mobile browsers.
